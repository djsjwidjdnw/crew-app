Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 2811959 ALBERTA LTD. ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the Crew mobile application and website ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account InformationName, email address, phone number, password
Profile InformationTrade type, experience level, years in field, bio, location, profile photo
CertificationsCertification names, expiry dates, certification photos
Job InformationJob titles, descriptions, pay rates, locations, duration (for Journeymen posting jobs)
Usage DataSwipe activity, matches, messages sent and received
Location DataGPS coordinates (with your permission) for distance-based filtering
Device InformationDevice type, operating system, app version

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Display your profile to other users for matching purposes
  • Enable in-app messaging between matched users
  • Provide location-based filtering of profiles and jobs
  • Improve the App and develop new features
  • Send you important notifications about your account, matches, and messages
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and prevent misuse

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in these circumstances:

  • With other users: Your profile information (name, trade, experience, bio, location, certifications, profile photo) is visible to other users of the App for matching purposes. Your exact GPS coordinates are never shared; only approximate distances are displayed.
  • With service providers: We use Supabase for data storage and authentication, and Vercel for web hosting. These providers process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • When required by law: We may disclose information in response to a valid legal request from law enforcement or government authorities.

4. Location Data

We request access to your device's location to provide distance-based filtering of profiles and jobs. Location access is optional. If you deny location permission, the App will default to a general Alberta location. You can change location permissions in your device settings at any time.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase, located in Canada (ca-central-1 region). We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS), row-level security policies on our database, and secure authentication. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or regulatory purposes. Messages may be retained for safety review purposes for up to 90 days after account deletion.

7. Your Rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by us
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data (see Deleting Your Account)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing (which may limit App functionality)
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have collected data from a person under 18, we will delete it promptly.

9. Photos and Uploaded Content

Profile photos and certification photos you upload are stored in secure cloud storage. These images are visible to other users of the App. You can delete uploaded photos at any time through your profile settings.

10. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

12. Deleting Your Account

In the app: open Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete account again. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

By email: if you can't use the app, email privacy@crewapp.ca or support@crewapp.ca from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it.

What is deleted:

  • Your login (email and password)
  • Your profile: name, phone number, location, bio, trade, experience, availability and profile photo
  • Your certifications and certification photos
  • Your swipes, matches and the messages in those conversations
  • Endorsements and ratings you gave or received
  • Jobs you posted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Section 6 (Data Retention) explains the limited cases where we may keep information after an account is deleted.
